GEOMETRY & APPROXIMATIONS

The 3-DOF model resolves chassis heave + pitch + arm rotation, mass/inertia, gravity, spring torque, viscous damping and unilateral tire/terrain contact. A pitch restraint represents the rest of the vehicle’s support. Wheel radius is used to bridge narrow potholes. Not a fatigue, traction, tire-flex or certified structural model.
Engineering interpretation: Sprung mass is the load supported by this suspension station, not necessarily the full 150 kg AMR payload. At reset, the model calculates spring preload for a static equilibrium on flat ground. Chassis pitch is free to respond, with a damped restraint representing the other wheel stations that are outside this one-wheel model. The extra parallel arm is an ideal equal-length parallelogram guide; its additional mass and joint friction are not yet included. This is a planar concept-screening simulator, not an arbitrary multibody solver. Test extreme cases with a CAD/multibody or FEM package before committing geometry.
